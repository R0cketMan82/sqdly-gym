# SQDLY Gym user manual

## Try the shared profile in Preview

1. Open **SQDLY Gym Dev** on your phone and connect it to the Gym development server.
2. Sign in with the email and password for an existing SQDLY **Preview** account.
3. Compare the display name, member number, and photo with your SQDLY Preview
   profile. Gym also shows the saved location and language when available.
4. Choose **Sign out** when finished. This signs out the current Gym session.

If the profile cannot load, check your connection and choose **Retry**.
Closing the app completely clears this development session. Sign up, password
recovery, and profile edits are handled by the existing SQDLY Preview flows
at this stage.
